Design the homepage of Circle, a peer-to-peer rental marketplace where people can rent items from other people and earn money by listing their own unused items.

The attached hand-drawn wireframe is the exact layout reference. Preserve its structure, proportions, hierarchy, spacing, and number of sections. Do not redesign the page architecture.

The goal is to turn the wireframe into a polished, modern marketplace interface while keeping the exact same layout.

Brand

Application name:

Circle

Core concept:

Rent what you need. Earn from what you own.

Circle connects people who have things they are not currently using with people who only need those things temporarily.

Examples of rentable items:

- Cameras
- Laptops
- Tools
- Gaming equipment
- Party equipment
- Sports equipment
- Furniture
- Electronics
- Camping equipment
- Cars/bikes and other suitable items

The interface should communicate:

- Peer-to-peer sharing
- Trust
- Convenience
- Affordability
- Local discovery
- Temporary access instead of buying

Overall Visual Style

Use a minimal, premium marketplace aesthetic.

- Clean white/light background
- Dark black typography
- Subtle gray borders
- Minimal shadows
- Slightly rounded corners
- Generous whitespace
- Modern typography
- Strong visual hierarchy
- No gradients
- No unnecessary decorative elements
- No excessive colors

The design should feel like a serious modern marketplace rather than a social-media application.

Use Circle as the main brand name throughout the interface.

Important Design Rules

Do not change the wireframe structure.

Do not add:

- Testimonials
- Statistics
- Large hero illustrations
- Promotional banners
- Blog sections
- Extra categories
- Pricing tables
- Separate CTA sections

The homepage should immediately communicate that Circle is a marketplace for renting things from other people.

The most important interaction is the search bar.

The most important visual component is the rental listing card.

Create the listing cards as reusable Figma components with variants for:

- Default
- Hover
- Saved/favorited

Use Auto Layout throughout the design.

Create reusable components for:

- Header
- Search bar
- Listing card
- Section header
- Footer
- Navigation links

Make the design responsive while preserving the original information hierarchy.

The final result should look like a real, production-ready peer-to-peer rental marketplace called Circle, while remaining visually faithful to the supplied hand-drawn wireframe.
